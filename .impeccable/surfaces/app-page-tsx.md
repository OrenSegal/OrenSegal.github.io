# Surface: app/page.tsx (full site — Hero, About, Projects, Contact, Footer, Nav)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: recruiters + technical interviewers, split evenly between a fast skim and a deep read. Job: leave convinced Oren builds trustworthy AI infrastructure, and click into at least one real repo. Proof/content: 8 real shipped GitHub projects (lib/projects.ts), real contact links, no invented metrics. Constraint: Next.js 14 static export to GitHub Pages, no backend, no image generation available (code-led only).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a night flight-deck, not a SaaS hero — trust is earned by instruments that cross-check each other and stay calm under load, refusing the glowing-dashboard/hype-metric arrangement every AI portfolio ships.

OWN-WORLD: Matte near-black panel ground (not slate-950-purple), luminous white/warm-white instrument markings, one restrained radium-green accent for "live/verified" state and amber only for caution — never used decoratively. Aviation numerals (a real technical/mono face, not Inter-as-display) on round or rectangular gauge-faced cards; terse placard-caps labels beneath each. Hairline bezels, no glass/blur, no gradient fills.

STORY: Visitor scans a six-pack of calm instruments (positioning + proof cross-checked at a glance) → reads the pilot's log (About, in placard voice) → inspects the instrument card for each of the 8 real projects, each with a plate-numbered gauge reading its category → reaches the radio panel (Contact) to make contact.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A fixed six-instrument panel replaces the typewriter-hero: gauges for Projects(8), Skills(6), Open Source(100%), plus three identity dials (Role, Location, Focus) arranged in two ranked rows of three, needles resting at their true values on load (a small settle animation, not a bounce), name/title as placard caption beneath the panel, one small "cleared for contact" primary action.

FORM: Own top-ranked grounded candidate coincided with dealt challenger `signals-instruments-night-flight-six-pack`, which won on both audience identification and product clarity against my assigned candidate #5 (field-guide/specimen catalog) — verdict: WINS, becomes the build. Declined challengers: variable-font-specimen, moon-shadow-bazaar, curved-crease-shell, ukiyoe-block-registration, tropicalia-collage — each loses audience identification for a technical-hire audience. Raise from curved-crease-shell (declined): structural honesty in composition — no ornament that isn't load-bearing to a reading. Seed key: 7e5b9d73.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
