# Surface: app/page.tsx (full site: Hero, Shelfie, Work tiles, About, Contact, Nav, Footer; project and writing pages inherit)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: recruiters and technical interviewers for Applied AI roles, split between a fast skim and a deep read. Job: leave convinced Oren builds LLM products and the measurement that proves them, and click into at least one real repo. Proof: 12 public repos with CI badges and real last-push dates (lib/projects.ts, lib/project-stats.json), the 525-run benchmark, Shelfie as described in cv.md (closed beta, no user numbers). Constraint: Next.js 14 static export to GitHub Pages, code-led, no image generation. Never say Shelfie was "built with Claude Code agents"; never "production" or "at scale" for Shelfie; no invented metrics.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The type is the interface. One world owns the page, Metro's typographic tiles: pure black, giant light lowercase headlines cropped off the right edge, flat colour tiles as the only doors, zero chrome. It takes one discipline from the struck cathode stack: every alternative is present as an unlit ghost and the one worth choosing is struck forward in one glow. It refuses the hero-sentence-plus-card-list the category ships and the dark-plus-neon-edges template.

OWN-WORLD: Pure black ground. Ink is white; ghosts are white at low opacity. One live colour, sodium orange (#ff8c1a), owns every struck state: the chosen benchmark row, the hovered tile, focus, selection. One flat field colour, transit cobalt (#1b3ac4), floods a few tiles as content, never as decoration. No gradients, shadows, blurs, borders or rules; grouping is tile adjacency and the black gutter. Urbanist Light for every giant headline, Urbanist for prose, JetBrains Mono only for machine-sourced figures (dates, costs, pass rates). Recognisable with all content removed: black, a cropped giant lowercase word, a grid of flat tiles, one orange strike.

STORY: A visitor reads "applied ai engineer" sliding off the edge, then the one sentence of the offer, then the struck stack: seven real benchmark settings as ghosts with the cheapest-equal one lit, which is the whole eval argument in one tile. They see Shelfie as a flooded cobalt tile with its four real differentiators, then the twelve projects as live tiles, each with a two-sentence blurb, the real last-push date, and where the repo publishes one, a small figure copied from it. They click a tile. About and contact close the page in the same grid.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Masthead is one row of lowercase words. Below it the giant headline "applied ai engineer" at 12.5vw on desktop (19vw, two lines, on phones), light weight, deliberately cropped off the right edge: at 12.5vw the crop lands on the last letters of "engineer" at 1280-1600 wide, which is Metro's one-letter crop; 18-22vw would cut the word in half. Under it, in one measure, the offer: "I build LLM products in Python, TypeScript and Swift, and the evals that show whether they work." Three struck words as actions: read the benchmark, resume, github. The tile grid begins inside the first viewport: the 2x2 struck-stack tile (25x lit, seven ghost rows) on the left, the cobalt Shelfie tile beside it.

FORM: Metro Typographic Tiles (catalog digital-design-canon-metro-typographic-tiles), a bolder-register challenger that won both axes against the dealt leader; fused with Struck Cathode Stack (operate-b-struck-cathode-gauze) at the user's request, as one discipline (ghost/struck state), not a second world. Seed key f82b06b0, re-roll round 1, bolder register. Translation: Metro's sideways panorama becomes vertical sections for mobile and skim, with the cropped headline kept as the "next pane" promise. Signature interaction: on hover and focus a live tile's title is struck (orange, the one glow) and its foot swaps the category and date line for the verified strip, the live CI badge and the link; nothing the visitor is reading moves (an earlier flip was removed at the user's request because it hid the description). Tiles whose repos publish a figure carry a small data demonstration copied from that source (rootcause's 300-run comparison, litmus's 0 of 12, the retrieval eval's MRR interval, signal-scout's 3 planted bugs); projects without a published figure get none. The struck stack lights a row on hover or focus and returns the chosen row to lit on leave. Motion grammar: one ease, the headline glides in from the right edge, colour and opacity transitions only, a 1% press scale, nothing bounces, reduced motion disables all of it.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
